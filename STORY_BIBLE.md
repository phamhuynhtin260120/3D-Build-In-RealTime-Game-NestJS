# Story Bible

## Ten tam thoi

Tro Tan Cua Loi May

## The loai va huong game

Game hanh dong realtime top-down, co the phat trien theo huong co-op sinh ton, arena multiplayer, hoac dungeon nho theo tung man.

Trong phien ban hien tai, gameplay nen uu tien:

- Nguoi choi xuat hien tai spawn point trong mot dau truong.
- Di chuyen, chien dau, tranh diem nang luong, song sot qua cac dot quai.
- Multiplayer la diem manh: moi nguoi choi la mot chien binh duoc trieu hoi vao cung mot vung phong an.
- Cot truyen duoc mo khoa qua mission ngan, boss, vat pham, va mo ta man choi thay vi cutscene dai.

## Logline

Sau khi Loi May co dai bi vo tan trong vung dat dung nham, nhung chien binh go co linh hon duoc danh thuc de tien vao cac dau truong phong an, danh bai yeu quai Nhat bi dung nham hoa, thu hoi cac manh loi, va quyet dinh lieu nen cuu the gioi cu hay pha huy no hoan toan.

## Boi canh the gioi

Ngay xua co mot vuong quoc co ten Karakuri no Kuni, noi con nguoi, may moc go, va linh the cung ton tai. De bao ve vuong quoc khoi yeu quai, cac tho ren da tao ra Loi May: mot trai tim nang luong co the cap su song cho cac chien binh go.

Loi May ban dau la cong cu bao ve. Ve sau, no bi dung de phong an yeu quai vao cac vung te le. Khi chien tranh leo thang, vuong quoc ep qua nhieu linh hon vao Loi May, khien no qua tai va no tung. Cac manh vo roi xuong long dat, bien ca vung dat thanh mot dia gioi dung nham.

Hang tram nam sau, cac phong an suy yeu. Yeu quai tro lai. Nhung chien binh go con sot lai tinh day, nhung ho khong con ky uc day du. Moi chien binh chi nghe thay mot menh lenh lap lai trong Loi May:

"Thu hoi cac manh vo. Dong lai trai tim. Hoac dot chay tat ca."

## The gioi hien tai

The gioi game duoc chia thanh cac vung phong an:

- Dau Truong Tro: khu vuc huan luyen va spawn dau tien.
- Den Tho Say: noi te le da bi dung nham nuot, phu hop voi asset Altar of Penance.
- Vach Nui Than Khoi: vung nui da, khoi, cau thang vo, phu hop voi Inferno World.
- Rung Co Khi Chay Den: noi cac robot go bi hu hong lang thang.
- Cong Troi Tengu: dau truong tren cac platform cao, boss Tengu.
- Tam Loi Do: man cuoi, noi cac manh Loi May tap hop.

## Nhan vat nguoi choi

Nguoi choi la Moc Co, chien binh go duoc tao tu than cay linh va khung may co dai.

Moc Co khong phai anh hung hoan hao. Ho la vu khi bi bo lai sau mot cuoc chien sai lam. Dieu lam ho dac biet la moi lan hoi sinh tai spawn point, ho mat di mot phan ky uc cu nhung giu lai ban nang chien dau.

Ly do multiplayer ton tai trong lore:

- Moi nguoi choi la mot Moc Co khac nhau duoc danh thuc cung luc.
- Tat ca cung ket noi voi Loi May, nen co the nhin thay cung mot the gioi.
- Cac spawn point la "cot neo linh hon", giup Moc Co tai tao than xac sau khi bi ha guc.

## Phe va nhan vat

### Moc Co

Phe cua nguoi choi. Gom cac robot go nhu Voletir, Volepik, Roulepik. Day la cac chien binh co khi co linh hon, tung duoc tao de bao ve vuong quoc.

Vai tro gameplay:

- Player character.
- Dong minh NPC.
- Mini-boss phien ban bi hong.

### Yeu Linh Dung Nham

Yeu quai bi phong an qua lau, bi bien dang boi nhiet va loi nang luong.

Asset phu hop:

- Red Monster: quai can chien/manh.
- Aosaginohi: yeu linh lua/ao anh.
- Tengu: boss bay/nhanh.
- Altar of Penance: thuc the te le hoac boss phong an.

### Tan Du Ky Su

Nhom tho may cu da tao ra Loi May. Da bien mat, chi con lai bia da, may moc hong, va cac thong diep ngan.

Vai tro gameplay:

- Giai thich lore qua collectible.
- Tao ly do cho puzzle, cua khoa, vat pham nang cap.

## Nhan vat/boss chinh

### Aosaginohi - Linh Lua Ao Anh

Mot yeu linh lua co kha nang tao ao giac. No canh giu ky uc dau tien cua Loi May.

Vai tro:

- Boss dau hoac mini-boss.
- Chu de: su that bi che giau.

### Altar of Penance - Ban Tho Chiu Toi

Mot ban tho song, hap thu loi cua nhung nguoi tung hy sinh linh hon de tao Loi May.

Vai tro:

- Boss phong thu.
- Co the trieu hoi quai nho.
- Chu de: cai gia cua viec tao ra vu khi.

### Red Monster - Ke An Tro

Quai vat duoc sinh ra tu nhung manh Loi May bi nung chay. No khong co ly tri, chi an nang luong.

Vai tro:

- Enemy chinh trong wave.
- Boss can chien.

### Tengu - Su Gia Tren Cong Troi

Tengu tung la nguoi canh cong phong an. Sau khi Loi May vo, no tin rang moi Moc Co deu la toi do cua vuong quoc cu.

Vai tro:

- Boss co tinh cach ro.
- Khong hoan toan ac.
- Sau khi bi danh bai, no tiet lo su that ve Loi May.

## Mach truyen chinh

### Hoi 1: Thuc Tinh

Nguoi choi tinh day trong Dau Truong Tro. Khong co ky uc, chi co lenh thu hoi manh Loi May. Spawn point dau tien kich hoat.

Muc tieu gameplay:

- Hoc di chuyen.
- Hoc chien dau.
- Song sot wave dau.
- Thu manh Loi May dau tien.

### Hoi 2: Di Tich Va Loi Noi Doi

Nguoi choi tien vao Den Tho Say va gap Aosaginohi. Cac manh ky uc cho thay Moc Co khong chi la chien binh bao ve, ma tung duoc dung de dan ap yeu quai va ca con nguoi phan doi Loi May.

Muc tieu gameplay:

- Mo khoa enemy moi.
- Them mission thu thap ky uc.
- Boss Aosaginohi.

### Hoi 3: Toi Cua Nguoi Tao Ra

Nguoi choi den Altar of Penance. Tai day, linh hon trong Loi May cau xin duoc giai thoat. Tengu xuat hien va canh bao rang viec khoi phuc Loi May co the lap lai tham hoa.

Muc tieu gameplay:

- Defend objective.
- Chien dau quanh ban tho.
- Chon cuu hoac pha cac cot phong an phu.

### Hoi 4: Cong Troi Tengu

Nguoi choi doi dau Tengu tren cac platform cao. Sau tran dau, Tengu noi ro: Loi May khong phai vat cuu the gioi. No la nha tu linh hon.

Muc tieu gameplay:

- Boss nhanh, co bay/teleport neu co the.
- Map co nhieu vuc, platform, lava.
- Mo khoa su that cot truyen.

### Hoi 5: Tam Loi Do

Nguoi choi den trung tam dia gioi. Cac manh Loi May tu tap, mo ra lua chon cuoi:

- Khoi phuc Loi May: the gioi on dinh hon, nhung linh hon tiep tuc bi giam.
- Pha huy Loi May: linh hon duoc giai thoat, nhung the gioi cu sup do hoan toan.
- Tai tao Loi May moi: can nhieu Moc Co cung hy sinh ky uc de tao mot trai tim khong con giam giu linh hon.

## Cau truc mission de build

### Mission 1: Troi Day Tai Dau Truong Tro

Noi dung:

- Player spawn.
- Server tao world state co player va spawn point.
- Enemy chua can co neu chua co combat.
- Muc tieu la di chuyen den 3 diem nang luong.

Muc dich:

- Hop voi tinh trang project hien tai.
- Co the build tren GameClient hien co.

### Mission 2: Thu Hoi Manh Loi Dau Tien

Noi dung:

- Them object "Core Fragment".
- Player cham vao de thu thap.
- Khi tat ca fragment duoc thu thap, mission complete.

### Mission 3: Dot Quai Dau Tien

Noi dung:

- Red Monster xuat hien theo wave.
- Player song sot trong thoi gian ngan.

### Mission 4: Ban Tho Say

Noi dung:

- Altar of Penance lam objective hoac boss.
- Player phai kich hoat/cat dut 3 cot phong an.

### Mission 5: Tengu Phan Xu

Noi dung:

- Tengu la boss co loi thoai.
- Sau khi thua, Tengu tro thanh nguoi tiet lo thong tin.

## Huong art va level

Nen dung 3 lop hinh anh:

- Robot go: dai dien cho nguoi choi va cong nghe co dai.
- Dung nham/di tich: dai dien cho the gioi sau tham hoa.
- Yeu quai Nhat: dai dien cho linh hon bi phong an va hau qua cua Loi May.

Khong nen viet cot truyen lech thanh sci-fi hien dai, vi asset hien co manh hon o huong fantasy co khi co dai.

## Nguyen tac viet tiep

- Moi asset lon nen co vai tro trong lore.
- Moi boss phai dai dien cho mot su that ve Loi May.
- Story nen ngan gon trong gameplay, khong dua vao cutscene dai.
- Multiplayer phai duoc giai thich bang spawn point va ket noi Loi May.
- Neu them tinh nang moi, nen gan no voi lore: hoi sinh, fragment, wave, boss, upgrade, quest.
